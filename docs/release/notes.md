DuoLid 0.9.0-beta.1 adds a configurable lid-driven folding desktop, top-to-bottom blur, soft edge bleed, optional pastel corner glow, and an original magnetic opening sound.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Universal for Apple silicon and Intel; automatic effects require a compatible lid-angle sensor and Screen Recording access. Unsupported hardware can use the sample preview. This beta uses SDR capture; HDR and protected content may look different.

The effect starts at 62° by default. Colorful glow is optional and off initially. Your existing preferences are preserved. Updates are signed, hosted on GitHub, and installed with your interaction.
